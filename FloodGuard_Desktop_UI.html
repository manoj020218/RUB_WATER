Why FloodGuard by Jenix?

Built to government audit standards — every alert, every action, fully accountable

⚡
Real-Time Monitoring

Water level updates every 2 seconds during danger events. RS485 ultrasonic sensor with 5-reading filtered average eliminates false alarms from vehicle splashes, animals, or debris.

🔴
Instant Multi-Channel Alerts

FCM push notifications to all authorized Android and PWA users simultaneously. Siren, beacon, and prerecorded voice announcement trigger locally within 2 seconds of confirmed danger.

🔆
Solar Powered — Zero Downtime

Independent solar panel with battery backup ensures continuous operation through monsoon, grid failure, and extreme weather conditions — exactly when flood risk is highest.

📶
Offline Resilient Architecture

ESP32-S3 runs complete local safety logic independently. Internet loss does not affect siren, beacon, or voice announcement. Offline events sync automatically when connectivity returns.

📋
Complete Audit Trail

Every action — mute, force-clear, dry-run, config change — logged with user ID, login ID, session ID, device name, IP address, and timestamp. Meets government accountability requirements.

🏢
Multi-Location Management

Vendor-level, department-level, and location-level hierarchy with role-based permissions. Monitor hundreds of RUB sites from a single dashboard with region and division filters.
