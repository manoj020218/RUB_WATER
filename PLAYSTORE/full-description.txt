FloodGuard by Jenix is the operator app for the FloodGuard water logging alarm system, installed at railway underbridges (RUBs), road underpasses and low-lying roads. It gives field staff, municipal teams and railway authorities a live view of water levels and alarm status at every monitored site, so they can act before a vehicle drives into flooded water.

FloodGuard is used by organisations that have FloodGuard hardware installed. Accounts are issued by your organisation's administrator; there is no public sign-up.

LIVE MONITORING
• Live water level at each site from RS485 ultrasonic and switch-type level sensors
• Alarm status at a glance: Normal, Orange (alert) and Danger
• Device health: online/offline, battery, signal strength and sensor status
• Live sensor input mode and current configuration of each unit

INSTANT ALERTS
• Push notifications when a site crosses the Orange or Danger level, and when it clears
• Alerts only for the locations assigned to you

REMOTE CONTROL
• Mute or clear on-site sirens and beacons remotely (by role)
• View the alarm action sheet: which relays switch on at each level, and for how long

SITE CONFIGURATION (ADMIN)
• Set Orange, Danger and Clear levels, trigger and clear delays
• Choose sensor input mode: RS485 ultrasonic, switch-type, or both
• Capture the current RS485 reading as the zero level
• Save via cloud, or directly over the local network, with confirmation from the device

INSTALLATION TOOLS
• Find and set up a new FloodGuard unit over Bluetooth or the local Wi-Fi network
• Register devices and bind them to locations

REPORTS AND ACCOUNTABILITY
• Alarm history, incident reports and a full audit trail of every action
• Export reports for review
• Raise and track complaints for a site

USERS AND ACCESS
• Role-based access for administrators, operators and viewers
• Assign locations to users, activate/deactivate accounts and reset passwords

PRIVACY
FloodGuard does not show ads and does not sell your data. Location and Bluetooth permissions are used only during device installation to find a FloodGuard unit and read the Wi-Fi network name; your location is not recorded or sent to our servers. See our privacy policy at https://floodguard.jenix.in/privacy.html.

Also available as a web dashboard at https://app.floodguard.jenix.in.
