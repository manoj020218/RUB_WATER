**Why does that happen on GPIO3?**

#### Evidence 1: GPIO3 is a Strapping Pin with NO Internal Pull-Up
From the ESP32-S3 Technical Reference Manual, **GPIO3** (`JTAG_SEL`) is a strapping pin that is **floating at boot** (no internal pull-up or pull-down). It is not guaranteed to be in a stable logic state during normal operation.

#### Evidence 2: GPIO3 is Shared with the USB-to-UART Chip
On almost every ESP32-S3 dev board with a USB-serial converter (CP2102 or CH340), **GPIO3 is wired as the UART0 RX pin**. That means the USB chip is physically connected to the same pad. That chip has its own impedance and can load the signal, especially during multi-byte reads. It corrupts the data line, causing the library to fail the verification read.
