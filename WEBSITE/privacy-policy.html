Privacy Policy

Effective date: 17 May 2026

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how FloodGuard by Jenix collects, uses, stores, and protects information in the web dashboard, PWA, Android app, backend APIs, and device telemetry workflows.

2. Information We Process

  • User account data: login ID, role, assigned location scope, and authentication records
  • Session metadata: session ID, app type, device name, and last seen timestamp
  • Operational telemetry: water level, sensor state, switch state, power and network indicators
  • Control action logs: mute, force clear, dry run, and configuration changes
  • Notification data: FCM token and delivery event metadata
  • Network diagnostics: Wi-Fi, gateway reachability, internet status, and SIM health fields (when enabled)

3. Why We Process This Data

  • Provide real-time flood monitoring and alert distribution
  • Maintain audit and compliance evidence for infrastructure operations
  • Support role-based command authorization and incident forensics
  • Improve platform stability, device uptime, and emergency response quality

4. Data Retention

Retention policy may vary by deployment agreement. The default operational model can include compressed daily summaries for dry days and detailed retention for water-detection periods and incidents.

If no water is detected in a 24-hour period, operators may keep one summarized record marked as a no-water day and purge high-frequency dry telemetry according to approved policy.

5. Security Controls

  • JWT-based authenticated APIs and role access controls
  • Password hashing (bcrypt or equivalent secure algorithm)
  • Command authorization checks before high-risk actions
  • Audit logging for sensitive operations
  • Transport security for internet-facing endpoints where available

6. Data Sharing

Data is shared only with authorized project stakeholders, operators, and approved service providers required for platform operation. Jenix does not sell personal data under this project model.

7. User and Organization Rights

Authorized organizations can request correction of inaccurate account metadata, deactivation of users, and scoped data exports where technically and legally feasible.

8. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as platform scope, legal obligations, or deployment controls evolve. Updated versions should include a revised effective date.

9. Contact

For privacy and compliance queries, contact the authorized FloodGuard deployment administrator or Jenix support contact defined in your deployment agreement.

This document is a project policy template and should be reviewed by legal counsel before production rollout.
