About FloodGuard by Jenix

Jenix FloodGuard - RUB Water Level Monitoring and Alarm System

Mission

FloodGuard by Jenix is designed to reduce risk at Railway Under Bridges and low-lying road crossings by combining local emergency control with real-time remote monitoring.

Core Safety Design

The local controller is the safety authority. If cloud, VPS, or internet connectivity fails, on-site alarm decisions and relay activation continue based on sensor and switch state. This design minimizes dependency risk during peak flood events.

Platform Components

  • ESP32-S3 controller with RS485 ultrasonic water level sensor integration
  • Backup 300 mm and 500 mm level switch inputs
  • Local siren, beacon, and voice output relays
  • MQTT-first communication with HTTP fallback
  • Node.js backend and MongoDB event storage
  • Responsive web dashboard (PWA) and Android mobile app support
  • FCM notifications and complete incident audit logs

Operating Philosophy

Every operational event should answer: what happened, where it happened, when it happened, who acted, and what the sensor context was at that time. This creates accountability suitable for government and large infrastructure teams.

Current Scope

The current project scope includes firmware safety logic, backend APIs, role-based control, responsive PWA dashboard, Android packaging, notification handling, OTA strategy, and launch-ready legal pages.
