The Problem

Underpass Water Logging Kills Without Warning — Every Monsoon

Across India, hundreds of railway underbridges and urban underpasses flood during heavy rains. Without a water logging alarm system, drivers enter waterlogged underpasses unaware of the danger.

Normal

Water below 300 mm — passage safe

Alert Level

Water logging rising — drivers warned, operators notified

Danger Level

Water logging critical! On-site alarm + emergency notifications

🚗

Vehicles Swept Away by Water Logging

Two-wheelers, autos, and cars enter waterlogged underpasses unaware. Even 30 cm of fast-moving water can sweep a vehicle. No water logging alarm means no advance warning — which is the primary cause of these accidents.

🚷

No On-Site Water Logging Warning

Most underpasses have no water level sensor, no alarm, and no automated barrier. Manual patrolling is inconsistent — especially at night or during heavy rain when water logging develops quickly.

📵

Operators Unaware of Water Logging

Municipal workers and railway staff often learn about water logging only after accidents happen. There is no centralized system to monitor multiple underpass water logging incidents simultaneously.

📋

No Audit Trail

Without digital records of water logging events, it is impossible to prove response times or demonstrate compliance. Legal liability falls on the authority with no evidence of timely action.

How It Works

Water Logging Detected to Alarm Triggered in Under 5 Seconds

A rugged IoT controller at each underpass continuously measures water depth and instantly triggers alarms when water logging is detected.

1

Sensor Measures

Ultrasonic + RS485 sensors mounted at top of underpass measure water depth every few seconds with millimetre accuracy.

2

Controller Processes

ESP32-S3 controller evaluates water logging level against configured thresholds (Alert / Danger / Clear). Works offline too.

3

On-Site Alarm Fires

Loud siren + visual indicator activates instantly at the underpass when water logging is detected. No internet required for local alarm.

4

Cloud Notifies

Live telemetry pushed via MQTT to cloud. Operators receive instant in-app alerts about water logging and can view live dashboard.

5

Full Audit Trail

Every water logging event, alarm, acknowledgement, and action is timestamped and logged. Exportable reports for compliance.

Technical Specifications

Built for India's Field Conditions

Industrial-grade hardware with a modern cloud backend — designed to survive monsoons and power outages.

ComponentSpecification
ControllerESP32-S3 (Dual-core 240 MHz, Wi-Fi + BLE 5.0)
Primary SensorUltrasonic distance sensor (non-contact, mounted at top of underpass)
Secondary SensorRS485 water level transducer (redundancy + cross-validation)
ConnectivityWi-Fi (2.4 GHz) — SIM/cellular module optional
ProtocolMQTT over TCP (primary) + HTTP REST fallback
Cloud BackendNode.js REST API + MQTT broker — hosted on dedicated VPS
DashboardProgressive Web App (PWA) — works on any smartphone/tablet/desktop
Data StorageMongoDB (cloud) + local EEPROM buffer (offline resilience)
Firmware UpdatesOver-the-Air (OTA) — zero field visit required
Power12V DC (solar-ready) with daily scheduled reboot at configurable time
Alarm OutputOn-site siren relay + cloud push notifications + in-app alerts
Measurement Range0–400 cm water depth / water logging level (configurable per site)
