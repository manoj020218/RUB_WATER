The Problem

Underpasses Flood Without Warning — Every Monsoon Season

Across India, hundreds of railway underbridges and urban underpasses flood during heavy rains. Without a real-time alert system, drivers enter waterlogged underpasses unaware of the danger.

Normal

Water below 300 mm — passage safe

Alert Level

Water rising — drivers warned, operators notified

Danger Level

Flood! On-site alarm + emergency notifications

🚗

Vehicles Swept Away

Two-wheelers, autos, and cars enter flooded underpasses unaware. Even 30 cm of fast-moving water can sweep a vehicle. Lack of advance warning is the primary cause of these accidents.

🚷

No On-Site Warning

Most underpasses have no water level sensor, no alarm, and no automated barrier. Manual patrolling is inconsistent — especially at night or during heavy rain when visibility is poor.

📵

Operators Unaware

Municipal workers and railway staff often learn about flooding only after accidents happen. There is no centralized monitoring system to track multiple underpasses simultaneously.

📋

No Audit Trail

Without digital records, it is impossible to prove response times or demonstrate compliance. Legal liability falls on the authority with no evidence of timely action.

How It Works

From Sensor to Alert in Under 5 Seconds

A rugged IoT controller at each site measures water depth continuously and talks to the cloud via cellular/Wi-Fi.

1

Sensor Measures

Ultrasonic + RS485 sensors mounted at top of underpass measure water depth every few seconds with millimetre accuracy.

2

Controller Processes

ESP32-S3 controller evaluates water level against configured thresholds (Alert / Danger / Clear). Works offline too.

3

On-Site Alarm Fires

Loud siren + visual indicator activates instantly at the underpass. No internet required for local alarm.

4

Cloud Notifies

Live telemetry pushed via MQTT to cloud. Operators receive instant in-app alerts and can view live dashboard.

5

Full Audit Trail

Every event, alarm, acknowledgement, and action is timestamped and logged. Exportable reports for compliance.
