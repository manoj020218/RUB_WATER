Delete Your FloodGuard Account

FloodGuard by Jenix — account and data deletion request

This page explains how users of the FloodGuard by Jenix app (developer: Jenix Technology) can request deletion of their account and associated personal data.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to jenixindia@gmail.com with the subject "FloodGuard account deletion request".
  2. Include your Login ID, your full name and the organisation that issued your account.
  3. Send the email from the address registered with your organisation, or ask your organisation's FloodGuard administrator to confirm the request.
  4. We verify the request and confirm by email once the account is deleted — within 30 days.

Your organisation's FloodGuard administrator can also deactivate your account immediately from the Users section of the app while the deletion request is processed.

Data that is deleted

  • Account details — login ID, name, role and password hash
  • Push notification (FCM) tokens and active login sessions
  • Location assignments linked to your account

Data that may be retained

  • Audit logs of operational actions (e.g. alarm mute, configuration changes) — kept for up to 365 days for safety and compliance, then deleted
  • Sensor telemetry from flood monitoring devices is not personal data and belongs to the operating organisation; it is kept for up to 90 days in detailed form
