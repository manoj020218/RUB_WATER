3. Purpose of the System

FloodGuard is an operational safety and monitoring tool designed to assist government authorities, municipal corporations, railways, and infrastructure managers in monitoring water levels at underpasses, railway underbridges (RUBs), and low-lying road stretches.

The system provides:

  • Real-time water level data from IoT sensors
  • Configurable alert and danger threshold notifications
  • On-site alarms (siren, beacon, voice announcement)
  • Remote alarm management via mobile app and dashboard
  • Audit trails and compliance reports
  • Push notifications via Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM)
